Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS.

What size businesses do you work with?+

Mostly small businesses and owner-led teams with an established service to sell. Many come to us when the brand, website, or way they handle new clients has fallen behind the business.

Where does Ismile work with clients?+

Ismile is a North American digital agency serving businesses across the U.S. and Canada. We are based in Toronto and run every engagement through a remote-first process.

How much does a project cost?+

It depends on what needs to be made. A website, a full identity, and a connected booking process are different jobs. We talk through the scope before quoting.

How long does a project take?+

The work, content, feedback, and any tools we need to connect all affect timing. We agree on a schedule before the project starts.

Why work with Ismile instead of separate freelancers?+

The person designing the site needs to know what happens when someone clicks "book." We look at the brand, pages, and follow-up together, so you are not left joining the pieces yourself.

Can I hire you for one service?+

Yes. A website, brand project, marketing work, or workflow cleanup can stand alone. We will not bundle in pieces you do not need.

What happens after the consultation?+

We talk through what you are trying to change and what is already working. If the project fits, we follow up with a proposed scope and quote.

What will you need from me?+

Usually: your service details, existing brand and website access, examples of your current client process, and timely feedback. We confirm the exact inputs in the project scope.

Is ongoing support available?+

Yes. Digital marketing, consulting, and virtual support can be scoped after launch or as a separate engagement, depending on what you need.
