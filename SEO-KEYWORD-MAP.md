## Utility pages

Privacy, terms, cookie, accessibility, data-request, and do-not-sell pages serve trust/compliance intent rather than keyword acquisition. Keep their current literal titles, self-referencing canonicals, and footer links. The 404 page remains `noindex, follow` and must return an actual HTTP 404 at the hosting layer.

## Cannibalization guardrails

- `/web-design.html` owns the broad web-design and development service topic; articles target questions and problems, then link back.
- `/digital-marketing.html` owns broad digital marketing; a future dedicated SEO page should exist only if Ismile can present a distinct scope, process, deliverables, and proof.
- `/systems-automations.html` owns automation and AI implementation; articles target education and decision support.
- `/legal-services.html` owns industry-specific legal services; `leo-law.html` remains portfolio proof/concept, not a second service landing page.
- Do not launch Toronto and U.S. copies of the same service page unless the offer, regulations, pricing, proof, or process is genuinely different.
