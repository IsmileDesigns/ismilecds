Timeline

Where it started

A STORY OF
GROWTH.

From self-taught experiments on dial-up to a full creative and digital studio, every chapter expanded the work without losing the instinct that started it.

  1. 1999

    THE FIRST CLICK.

    A Photoshop DVD and a dial-up connection. That was the beginning. Yazmine taught herself the software, learned HTML while building pages on BlackPlanet, and kept experimenting until every detail felt intentional.

  2. 2008

    TOUCH GRAPHICS.

    The first real client base was artists—especially musicians who needed their image to carry the same energy as their work. That era shaped the studio's instinct for treating a brand as a reputation, not just a look.

  3. 2017

    ISMILE DESIGNS.

    The work had outgrown the original name. Ismile Designs launched as a more focused studio—an identity that better reflected the level of the work and the direction it was heading.

  4. 2025

    ISMILE CDS.

    The studio evolved into its full scope: brand identity, websites, systems, and strategy working together. Not just how a business looks, but how the whole operation connects, communicates, and grows.
