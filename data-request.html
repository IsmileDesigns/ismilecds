LEGAL · GDPR / CCPA

DATA REQUEST.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

If you'd like to exercise your privacy rights — under Canada's PIPEDA, the EU/UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA, or another applicable law — you can submit a request here. As a small studio, we handle these personally and directly; there's no call center, just an email that reaches us.

Rights You Can Request

  • Access — a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction — fix inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion — have your personal information removed from our systems, subject to legal/accounting retention requirements.
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format.
  • Restriction / Objection — limit or object to how we process your information.
  • Opt out of analytics — handled instantly via Cookie Preferences in the footer, no request needed.

How to Submit a Request

Email hello@byismile.com with the subject line "Data Request (GDPR/CCPA)" and include:

  • The type of request (access, correction, deletion, portability, or other).
  • Your name and the email address associated with your data.
  • Any details that help us locate your information (e.g., approximate date you contacted us).

Clicking the email link above will pre-fill a template for you.

Verification

To protect your information, we may ask you to confirm details (like the email address you used to contact us) before fulfilling a request. This prevents someone else from requesting your data.

Response Time

We aim to respond to all data requests within 30 days. If we need more time due to the complexity of a request, we'll let you know.

No Cost

There is no fee to submit a data request or to receive a response, except in cases of manifestly unfounded, repetitive, or excessive requests, where permitted by law.

Authorized Agents

If you're a California resident submitting this request through an authorized agent, we may require proof of the agent's authorization and verification of your identity directly.
